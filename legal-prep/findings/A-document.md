# A. The Document: Power Integrations Executive Officer Benefits Agreements (public baseline)

Prepared 2026-10-07 from SEC EDGAR, CIK 0000833640. Research only. Public record only. Every fact is cited to a URL. Items I could not find are listed at the end.

Base URL for all exhibits: `https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/`. EDGAR 8-K list: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000833640&type=8-K

Naming used below:
- "EOBA" = Executive Officer Benefits Agreement.
- "Legacy form" = the 2014 amended-and-restated form (still used for new hires through January 2025).
- "Go-forward form" = the form introduced with Jennifer Lloyd (July 2025) and Nancy Erba (November 2025). The company's own 2026 proxy uses "Go-Forward EOBAs" and "Legacy EOBAs" the same way.

## 5. Features that look unusual versus typical public-company severance forms

Each item is an observation drawn from the documents cited. No advice is given.

1. **Company pays all arbitration and litigation costs, including the executive's attorneys fees, win or lose (legacy form Sec. 11).** The clause reads: "All costs and expenses of arbitration or litigation, including but not limited to reasonable attorneys fees and other costs reasonably incurred by the Executive, shall be paid by the Company." It uses the AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules, not employment rules, and names San Jose. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364025000120/powi-20250331xex10d8.htm. The go-forward EOBAs (Lloyd, Erba) have no dispute-resolution clause at all; Lloyd's offer letter carries a JAMS employment arbitration clause with the Company paying JAMS fees above court filing fees; Erba's offer letter has none. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364025000157/powi-20250630xex10d5.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364026000037/powi-20251231xex10d85.htm.
2. **Non-compete exists only as a condition to Retirement Benefits.** The legacy form has no standalone non-compete or non-solicit; "Competition" (Sec. 21(g)), which includes recruiting any Company employee, only terminates Retirement Benefits (Sec. 5(a)(ii)). The go-forward forms drop "Competition" entirely and condition Retirement Benefits on not working full time elsewhere and not materially breaching the Confidentiality Agreement (Sec. 4(a)(ii)). The company's 2025 and 2026 proxies state the legacy benefits "are coupled with non-competition obligations that might not be enforceable in the absence of additional consideration." Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364025000093/powi-20250317xdef14a.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364026000065/powi-20260413xdef14a.htm.
3. **Legacy change-of-control trigger includes a CEO departure.** "Termination Upon Change of Control" (legacy Sec. 21(w)) is met by a without-Cause termination or Good Reason resignation within 18 months after "the date that the person serving as of the Effective Date as Chief Executive Officer of the Company ceases to serve in such office." Balakrishnan ceased to be CEO when Lloyd started on July 21, 2025 (8-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364025000146/powi-20250711x8k.htm). The 2026 proxy describes this as "18 months post CEO termination" for Legacy EOBAs and says the go-forward form removed "inclusion of leadership changes in the 'good reason' definition." Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364026000065/powi-20260413xdef14a.htm.
4. **Legacy single-trigger vesting at maximum performance.** Legacy Sec. 2(b) vests 100% of Performance Stock Awards at the "maximum achievable Performance Level" immediately before any CoC, with no termination required; Sec. 2(a) vests 25% of time-based awards (50%/100% if not assumed). Cash severance is also measured at maximum bonus. The 2026 proxy calls these "legacy single-trigger features" that were eliminated going forward. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364025000120/powi-20250331xex10d8.htm ; proxy above.
5. **Short cure and notice periods for Good Reason.** Both forms give the Company only 20 days to cure after notice (legacy Sec. 21(i); go-forward Sec. 19(f)). Legacy notice window is one year; go-forward is 90 days.
6. **Legacy Good Reason pay-cut prong applies only "following a Change of Control".** Legacy Sec. 21(i)(i) ties a salary/bonus/benefit decrease to a post-CoC context; demotion, relocation and breach prongs apply anytime. The go-forward form applies the pay-cut prong anytime but excludes across-the-board cuts affecting a majority of senior executives.
7. **Erba form: 30-minute commute test for relocation; no cure period for Cause; moral-turpitude prong; Board interpretation "final and binding".** Lloyd's form uses 50 miles, has a 30-day cure for Cause, and lacks the Board-authority sentence. Sources: Secs. 19(f)(iii), 19(b), 10 of https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364026000037/powi-20251231xex10d86.htm.
8. **Erba Sec. 11 overrides award agreements.** It states the EOBA "provides for the only post-termination vesting Executive is entitled to receive" and that all other unvested Stock Awards (including annual-bonus PSUs) "shall be forfeited for no additional consideration". The legacy form (Sec. 13) expressly does not limit the equity plans. Same source.
9. **Legacy "Highest Annual Salary" lookback and maximum-bonus measurement.** Legacy severance uses the highest salary in the prior three years and the bonus "at the applicable maximum achievable Performance Level" (Sec. 21(b), 21(j)); go-forward uses current salary and target.
10. **Legacy tax gross-up on the health cash-out.** Legacy Sec. 20(d) adds "an additional amount such that after payment of all taxes, Executive retains an amount equal to the Company's aggregate cost" if COBRA cannot be provided. The 2026 proxy states "we do not provide any tax gross-up payments on severance benefits." Go-forward Sec. 18(c) has no gross-up. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364025000120/powi-20250331xex10d8.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364026000065/powi-20260413xdef14a.htm.
11. **Retirement health coverage to Medicare eligibility (legacy only).** Legacy Sec. 5(b)(iii) pays medical and dental premiums for the executive and dependents until Medicare eligibility, continuing to dependents after death. The 2026 proxy reports an actuarial present value for this benefit for Gupta ($41k) and Walker ($0). Go-forward forms have no retiree health benefit.
12. **New Executive one-year waiting period and 6-month/5-year tiers (legacy).** Legacy benefits for an executive with fewer than five years as an executive officer are 6 months / 50% and only after one year of service (Sec. 21(k)), unless waived (Ademmer 2013; Nayyar 2010 partial; Gupta and Yee 2022 deemed Senior). Go-forward forms have no tiering and no wait.
13. **Prorated bonus on non-CoC termination measured at "lesser of target or actual" (Erba and Balow).** Lloyd's is at target; legacy is at maximum.
14. **No stated term, no renewal, no mutual notice period.** Every version is open-ended, at-will, and amendable only by a signed writing; the go-forward forms require a Board member's signature for amendments (cover Sec. 2). Erba's cover Sec. 1 conditions eligibility on signing and commencing service.
15. **Internal inconsistency in the public record for "other executives".** The 2026 proxy footnote says go-forward benefits for executives other than CEO/CFO are "6 months current base salary and .5x target annual incentive," while the May 2026 Balow 8-K (SVP) describes one year of salary and 100% of target PSUs. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364026000065/powi-20260413xdef14a.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/833640/000083364026000076/powi-20260504x8k.htm.
16. **Section 280G best-net cutback in every version; no 280G gross-up at any point.** Legacy Sec. 8 and go-forward Sec. 7 use identical "Reduced Amount" mechanics, with the accountants appointed by the Company (subject to the executive's reasonable acceptance) and the Company bearing their cost.

1. Which form is "current" depends on the executive. As of the latest filings, legacy-form agreements remain in force for Walker (A&R May 1, 2014, as amended 2020 and 2025), Vikram Balakrishnan and Roland Saint-Pierre (Jan 28, 2025); go-forward forms exist for Lloyd and Erba; Balow's is described but unfiled. The 2026 proxy says go-forward terms will be used for all new agreements. Whether an existing legacy holder would be asked to replace a legacy agreement is not stated anywhere in the public record.
2. The 2026 proxy's "6 months / .5x" statement for other executives conflicts with the Balow 8-K's "one year / 100%". One of the two may be a drafting error, or Balow's SVP role may have been treated as CFO-equivalent. The record does not say.
3. The Erba document is titled "Senior Executive Officer Benefits Agreement" but the 10-K index calls it "Executive Officer Benefits Agreement" and the 8-K calls it an "executive officer benefits agreement". Whether "Senior" denotes a distinct tier in a new family of forms (for example, Senior vs. non-Senior go-forward forms) is not explained in any filing.
4. The Erba form's Sec. 3(a) non-CoC prorated bonus is at the "lesser of target ... or actual performance level measured as of the date of termination"; how "actual performance level" is measured mid-year is not defined in the agreement.
5. The Ademmer 2014 exhibit (Ex. 10.9) has several sections marked "[Reserved]" (COBRA continuation, retiree health, parachute, 409A other benefits, health cash-out) that are populated in the Nayyar 2014 exhibit (Ex. 10.8). I treat the Nayyar version as the representative US legacy form, but the filings do not explain why Ademmer's differs.
6. My reading of section numbers in the Lloyd and Erba forms relies on text extracted from inline XBRL HTML; the "1.", "2." page markers in the extracted text were distinguished from section numbers by context. Any single section citation should be checked against the HTML before relying on it.
7. The 2026 proxy summary table says legacy severance is "12 months highest base salary ... 1x annual incentive at maximum" for senior executives on a CoC termination. The agreement text actually provides 6 months lump sum plus "up to" 6 further months paid monthly only "until Executive secures new employment" (Sec. 3(a)(iv)(2)). The proxy's shorthand omits the mitigation condition.
8. Whether the legacy 18-month window tied to Balakrishnan's departure as CEO (July 21, 2025) is treated by the company as running (to about January 21, 2027) for legacy holders is implied by the proxy's "18 months post CEO termination" wording but is not stated expressly for any named executive.
