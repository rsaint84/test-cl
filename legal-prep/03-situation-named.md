## 6. The five questions, final form

1. **What do we have?** One agreement, filed, legacy form. What is in the new paper, word for word, and what does it say about the old one?
2. **What is the gap?** Clause by clause, old against new, with a dollar figure where one exists.
3. **What is the clock?** Six clocks above. Which ones the new paper would stop.
4. **Who decides?** Who drafted the new paper, who must approve it, who can waive the one-year wait, who signs.
5. **Who has stood here before?** Legacy holders asked to switch, at this company and at peers. Executives who refused. What happened to them.
