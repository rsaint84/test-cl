## 3. The one date that matters most

The legacy second trigger reads: within eighteen months after the person serving as CEO on the Effective Date ceases to serve in that office. Your Effective Date is January 28, 2025. That CEO left the office July 21, 2025. Eighteen months from then is about January 21, 2027. Whether this window is open for you, and what the new paper does to it, is not in the public record.

## 7. The five questions, refined

1. **What do we have?** What exact paper is on the table, and does it replace, amend, or end the January 2025 agreement?
2. **What is the gap?** Clause by clause, what does the new paper take from the legacy form and what does it give?
3. **What is the clock?** Which windows are open now: the CEO-departure trigger, the release deadline, any review period?
4. **Who is across the table?** Who drafted the paper, who approves it, and who signs for the company?
5. **Who has stood here before?** Which legacy holders at this company and at peers faced a swap, and what did they do?

Questions two through five are partly answered above. Question one needs the chat.
